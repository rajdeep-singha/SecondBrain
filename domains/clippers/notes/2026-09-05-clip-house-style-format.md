## Problem
Clip write-ups kept getting rejected on formatting: ellipses, quoted terms in
headlines, lowercase quote starts, extra `Caption:` lines. Each new episode
drifted from the house style already established in the folder's other files.

## Solution / code
Lay out every clip exactly like this:

```
Clip N : start–end  (≈Xs) — short tag

[Speaker]-led headline — the core point in plain words

"Full verbatim quote 1 pulled straight from the transcript"

"Full verbatim quote 2"
```

House-style rules (match the existing clip files, don't reinvent per episode):
- **Speaker-led headline**: "Marcus on why Robinhood's capital is durable" — not a
  vague descriptor.
- **No quotation marks in the headline**, ever (even around coined terms like
  "AI slop", "borrowed conviction").
- **No ellipsis anywhere** — no `...`, no `(...)`, in headlines or quotes. Never
  splice non-adjacent fragments; pick one **contiguous** stretch per quote.
- **Every quote starts capitalized** (pulls are mid-sentence); rest stays verbatim.
- **Verbatim, contiguous, word-for-word** — keep the transcript's real wording
  (even stutters/typos), just don't stitch across gaps.
- **No `Caption:` / `Why it works:` lines**, **no emojis**.
- **Chronological clips first, then a Ranking section** at the bottom ordered by
  viral / hot-take / controversy potential.

## Gotchas
- When unsure of the format, **read the folder's existing clip files** (e.g.
  `memefi-stocks-clips.md`) and match them — the house style lives there, not in
  your guess.
- Keep obvious transcription fixes *outside* the quotes (bracketed editor's note),
  or the quote stops being verbatim — see [[transcript-speaker-attribution]].
- Pairs with [[clip-selection-and-length-guardrails]].
